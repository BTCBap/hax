# Code Of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

People participating in this project are expected to:

- Be direct and respectful
- Assume good faith until there is evidence otherwise
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people
- Keep discussions technically grounded and actionable
- Respect different levels of experience

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Discriminatory or hateful conduct
- Deliberate disruption of discussion or review
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy.

Maintainers may also restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

For conduct problems, contact the maintainer through the repository profile.

For security problems, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
